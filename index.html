A SIMPLE USER GUIDE
How to use Pocket
＋ Add money

Record money you receive. Enter the person or source and amount. Add what it was for if you want.

− Expense

Record spending with just purpose and amount. Advanced options let you add category, date, payment method and notes.

↕ History

All incoming and outgoing records appear here in date order. Filter them, select a month, then tap any record to edit or delete.

◉ Available balance

Total received minus total spent. It shows the money left according to the records you have entered.

◔ Analysis

See monthly totals, net savings, category-wise expenses and a six-month spending trend.

◐ Themes

Tap the top-right theme button to cycle through Google-inspired light, lavender, mint, peach and dark themes. Your choice is remembered.

Demo data

On Home, load sample records to explore the app. Remove demo data whenever you are finished.

Privacy

Transactions are stored locally in this browser. Export a backup regularly; clearing browser data may erase them.

PERSONALISE YOUR APP
Themes & settings
Choose your colour theme

Colour themes change the accent colours across cards, buttons, charts and highlights. Day/night mode is controlled separately from the top bar.

Day / night appearance

Switch between a bright daytime interface and a darker night interface without changing your selected accent colour.

Backup data

Download or restore a JSON copy of your transactions.

Reset all data
